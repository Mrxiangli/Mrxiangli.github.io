# Side-view cat reference

`side-view-concept.png` is the design reference generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The running SVG adapts its rounded head, short muzzle, plump body, green eye, white socks and curved striped tail to the website's 32px animation cells.

`side-view.svg` is the actual right-facing running pose, exported by `python3 scripts/build-cat-sprites.py` for comparison with the concept.

Generation prompt:

Full body facing right, playful gentle running stride, all four short rounded white paws separately readable, visible curved upright ringed tail on left. A plump gray tabby and white domestic cat, broad white chest and belly, white socks, white muzzle and blaze up the nose, soft gray back with just three dark gray tabby stripes, pink tiny nose and ear interior, big soft sage-green eye. Oversized rounded head, small rounded triangular ears, short muzzle, rounded bean body, soft charcoal thin outlines, flat vector-like colors, very simple clean charming chibi illustration readable at 48 pixels. Not angular, not long snout, not realistic, not furry detail. Single character centered with generous margin on plain white background. No text, no accessories, no shadows.
